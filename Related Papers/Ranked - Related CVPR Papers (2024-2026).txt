TOP 20 MOST RELEVANT CVPR 2024–2026 PAPERS FOR SEN12MS-CR CLOUD REMOVAL PROJECT

1. Effective Cloud Removal for Remote Sensing Images by an Improved Mean-Reverting Denoising Model with Elucidated Design Space (EMRDM)
   Year: CVPR 2025
   Relevance: FR+++
   Reason: Direct remote-sensing cloud removal. Builds a direct cloudy-to-cloudless mean-reverting diffusion process with reformulated forward dynamics and an ODE-based backward process. Extremely close to the project task and forward/reverse modeling direction.

2. Residual Diffusion Bridge Model for Image Restoration (RDBM)
   Year: CVPR 2026
   Relevance: FR-Method+++
   Reason: General image restoration rather than cloud removal, but highly relevant mathematically. Develops generalized forward/reverse diffusion bridges and uses clean-degraded residuals to control restoration while preserving intact information.

3. Spatial-Spectral Residuals Informed Diffusion Neural Operator for Pan-sharpening
   Year: CVPR 2026
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Remote-sensing reconstruction combining diffusion, neural operators, and spatial-spectral residual constraints. Particularly important for PDE/operator-style modeling and scientific spectral fidelity.

4. EMR-Diff: Edge-aware Multimodal Residual Diffusion Model for Hyperspectral Image Super-resolution
   Year: CVPR 2026
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Multimodal remote-sensing reconstruction using residual diffusion. Jointly preserves spatial and spectral information and uses complementary modalities to reconstruct the desired observation.

5. Degradation-Robust Fusion: An Efficient Degradation-Aware Diffusion Framework for Multimodal Image Fusion in Arbitrary Degradation Scenarios
   Year: CVPR 2026
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Multimodal diffusion under degraded observations. Uses observation-model and fusion constraints during sampling, making it highly relevant to observation-supported SAR-optical reconstruction.

6. Bi-Bridge: Bidirectional Diffusion Bridges for Low-Light Image Enhancement
   Year: CVPR 2026
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Learns restoration and degradation directions together using bidirectional diffusion bridges. Strong conceptual connection to forward/reverse dynamics and cycle/round-trip consistency.

7. Bridging Remote Sensors with Multisensor Geospatial Foundation Models (msGFM)
   Year: CVPR 2024
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Learns jointly from multiple remote sensors including optical and microwave modalities. Supports paired/unpaired sensor data and includes cloud removal as a downstream task.

8. Self-Learning Hyperspectral and Multispectral Image Fusion via Adaptive Residual Guided Subspace Diffusion Model (ARGS-Diff)
   Year: CVPR 2025
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Multimodal spectral-spatial reconstruction using diffusion and residual-guided subspace decomposition. Relevant to preserving physically meaningful spectral structure.

9. Semantic-Adaptive Diffusion for Dynamic Spatiotemporal Fusion
   Year: CVPR 2026
   Relevance: SR+++
   Reason: Remote-sensing reconstruction using diffusion for spatiotemporal fusion while maintaining spectral consistency and spatial detail.

10. ReCoFuse: Ultra-Robust Image Fusion via Restorative Multi-Modal Diffusion Reciprocal Coupling
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR+++
    Reason: Jointly couples restoration and multimodal fusion rather than treating fusion as static conditioning. Relevant to dynamically integrating SAR information during cloud reconstruction.

11. Building Bridges across Spatial and Temporal Resolutions: Reference-Based Super-Resolution via Change Priors and Conditional Diffusion Model
    Year: CVPR 2024
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Remote-sensing conditional diffusion with mechanisms for determining when auxiliary/reference information should be trusted. Relevant to preventing inappropriate information transfer during SAR-guided reconstruction.

12. Dual Ascent Diffusion for Inverse Problems
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Treats diffusion-based reconstruction as a constrained inverse problem using MAP estimation and dual-ascent optimization. Relevant to explicit observation/data consistency.

13. HIR-Diff: Unsupervised Hyperspectral Image Restoration Via Improved Diffusion Models
    Year: CVPR 2024
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Hyperspectral restoration combining diffusion with low-rank and total-variation priors. Useful precedent for combining mathematical priors with learned generative restoration.

14. HalluGen: Synthesizing Realistic and Controllable Hallucinations for Evaluating Image Restoration
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++ (Evaluation)
    Reason: Directly studies hallucinated but perceptually realistic restoration outputs. Particularly relevant to scientific fidelity because visually plausible reconstructions may still be scientifically incorrect.

15. Hyperspectral Pansharpening via Diffusion Models with Iteratively Zero-Shot Guidance
    Year: CVPR 2025
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Uses measurement-guided diffusion for hyperspectral reconstruction while explicitly respecting spectral information. Relevant to controlling generative priors with actual observations.

16. Regulating Rather than Constraining: Adaptive Guidance for Complex Spectral Reconstruction in Pansharpening
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Focuses directly on spectral reconstruction and argues that overly rigid physical constraints can hurt heterogeneous spectral recovery. Relevant when designing conservation/spectral constraints.

17. UniFusion: A Unified Image Fusion Framework with Robust Representation and Source-Aware Preservation
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Uses source-aware preservation and reconstruction-alignment objectives to keep fused results consistent with original observations. Highly relevant to preventing unsupported SAR-optical fusion artifacts.

18. TESSERA: Temporal Embeddings of Surface Spectra for Earth Representation and Analysis
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Jointly models Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 and explicitly deals with irregular optical observations including cloud obstruction. Useful for S1/S2 representation design.

19. SARMAE: Masked Autoencoder for SAR Representation Learning
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: SAR-specific representation learning that accounts for speckle and uses paired optical information for semantic alignment. Relevant to improving the Sentinel-1 VV/VH branch.

20. Multi-Modal Image Fusion via Intervention-Stable Feature Learning
    Year: CVPR 2026
    Relevance: SR++
    Reason: Tests whether one modality genuinely provides complementary information using masking and modality interventions. Very useful for proving that SAR contributes real information to cloud reconstruction instead of acting as a generic auxiliary feature.
